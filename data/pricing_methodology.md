# Pricing Data Methodology

## Sources
- The Kitchn 2024 multi-store comparisons (TJ, Aldi, Walmart, Costco)
- Ramsey Solutions Aldi vs Walmart (May 2025)
- Consumer Reports basket comparison (late 2025)
- Washington Consumers' Checkbook (Jan 2026)
- Public store websites for verification

## Limitations
- Point-in-time snapshot (mid-2025). Prices change weekly.
- Regional variation 5-10%. Listed prices reflect mid-Atlantic / Midwest averages.
- TJ doesn't sell online; prices from traderjoesprices.com (Chicago store) + Reddit threads.
- Costco prices "normalized" to single-unit (their bulk packs divided down).
- "Industry estimates" used where exact item not in any comparison study.
- Publix prices are highest-cost reference (premium positioning, no value brand).

## Confidence levels
- High: items from named comparison studies (10 items)
- Medium: triangulated from multiple sources (10 items)
- Low: industry estimates only (5 items)

For production-grade analysis, would need direct store-level scraping with location-controlled samples (~$500-1000 in scraping costs).
